# naval-architecture-undergraduate-thesis

This repository contains my undergraduate thesis (Bachelor's thesis), completed in collaboration with B.Sc. Natan Moreno de Almeida Nunes da Silva and B.Sc. Rafael de Oliveira Souza.

Our project focused on the conceptual design of a 2,100-ton Offshore Patrol Vessel (OPV).

My main contributions to the project were:

**Technical Scope:**
- **Design Methodology**: Applied the University College London (UCL) ship design methodology to establish initial design parameters.
- **General Arrangement, Weights, and Centers**: Developed the initial layout, estimated weight distribution, and determined centers of gravity.
- **Hydrostatics and Stability**: Analyzed vessel behavior in calm water using Maxsurf Modeler and Stability.
- **Seakeeping**: Evaluated vessel behavior in waves, applying seakeeping theory and calculating Response Amplitude Operators (RAOs) using Maxsurf Motion.
- **Cost Assessment**: Estimated life-cycle costs for the vessel.

**Project Management & Leadership:**
- **Project Coordination**: Coordinated team tasks, tracked deadlines, and ensured alignment across all project stages.
- **Document Structuring & Benchmarking**: Structured the final thesis layout and analyzed previously approved projects to benchmark standards, formatting, and technical rigor.

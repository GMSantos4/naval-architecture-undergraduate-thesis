# Conceptual Design and Engineering Analysis of an Offshore Patrol Vessel (OPV)

This repository contains my undergraduate thesis (Bachelor's thesis) in Naval Engineering, completed in collaboration with B.Sc. Natan Moreno de Almeida Nunes da Silva and B.Sc. Rafael de Oliveira Souza.

The project focuses on the initial stage of ship design (conceptual design) for a **2,100-ton Offshore Patrol Vessel (OPV)**, establishing fundamental naval architecture parameters, hull form performance, structural arrangement, and economic feasibility.

## Technical Scope & Key Contributions

### Technical Scope
- **Design Methodology**: Applied the University College London (UCL) ship design methodology to establish initial design parameters and sizing iterations.
- **General Arrangement, Weights, and Centers**: Developed the initial internal layout and determined weight distribution, lightship weight breakdown, and center of gravity ($CG$) coordinates.
- **Hydrostatics & Stability**: Conducted hydrostatic calculations and intact/damaged stability analyses using **Maxsurf Modeler** and **Maxsurf Stability** to evaluate vessel performance in calm water.
- **Seakeeping**: Overview of seakeeping theory, analyzed vessel motions in irregular seaways, evaluating Response Amplitude Operators (RAOs) and operational limits in waves using **Maxsurf Motion**.
- **Cost Assessment**: Performed life-cycle cost estimation and economic assessment for initial procurement and operational phases.

### Project Management & Coordination
- **Project Coordination**: Managed team schedules, milestone deadlines, and task delegation to ensure timely project delivery.
- **Document Structuring & Benchmarking**: Structured the final thesis document and benchmarked formatting, methodology, and technical rigor against previously approved academic projects.

## Tools & Software Used

- **Naval Architecture & Stability**: Maxsurf Modeler, Maxsurf Stability, Maxsurf Motion.
- **Design Methodology**: UCL Ship Design Synthesis Methodology.
